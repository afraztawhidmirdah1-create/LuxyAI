Luxy AI Privacy Policy

Luxy AI is made by Afraz Studio. Last updated: October 2026.

No account

Luxy AI has no sign-in and no account. We do not run servers that store your chats.

What stays on your phone

If you choose "Save my chats on this device", your chats, notes, to-do list and settings are stored only on your phone. If you choose "Try with no history", chats are not saved and disappear when you close the app. You can delete chats or clear the app data at any time.

What is sent over the internet

To answer you, the text you type (and any photo or file you choose to attach) is sent to third-party services that provide AI answers, web knowledge, news, weather, currency rates, and picture generation. If you ask for weather, your city or approximate location is sent to the weather service. We do not sell your data and the app shows no ads.

Permissions

Internet: to get answers. Camera: only when you choose to take a photo. Microphone: only when you tap the mic for voice input. Location: optional, only for local weather.

Children

Luxy AI is not directed to children under 13.
